A FEW GOOD THINGS TO KNOW

Clear from
the start.

Useful details before your first sale.

Can I use it without an internet connection?+

After the app has loaded and cached on this device, core billing and inventory work offline. Online AI scanning and external sharing services still require a connection.

Where is my store data saved?+

Your products and sales are stored in this browser on this device. Export backups regularly in Store settings. Data does not automatically transfer between devices or different website addresses.

Does the app confirm UPI or card payments?+

Payments are recorded manually. Verify the transaction in your bank app or card terminal before marking a bill paid.

Can customers receive a PDF bill?+

Yes. Download, print, or share a branded PDF. Public receipt QR links need a separately configured receipt server; this GitHub Pages website alone does not host those receipts.

How does purchase invoice scanning work?+

Upload a supplier bill and review the extracted details before saving. Set your selling rate and quantity yourself. Gemini scanning needs your API key and internet; without a key, the app provides a labeled demo.
