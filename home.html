BEFORE YOU BEGIN
How are payments confirmed?+

Payments are recorded manually. Verify UPI or card payments in your bank app or terminal before marking a sale paid.

Can I share PDF receipts and QR links?+

You can download, print, or share a PDF receipt. Public receipt QR links require a separate receipt server; GitHub Pages alone does not host customer receipts.

What needs an internet connection?+

Online AI invoice scanning needs an API key and internet access. Without a key, the scanner provides a labeled demo. External sharing services need connectivity. Core billing works offline after caching.
